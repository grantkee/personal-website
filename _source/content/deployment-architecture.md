### High-Impact Accomplishments

- **Architected and implemented a multi-signature deployment strategy for EVM infrastructure**, eliminating reliance on single hardware keys for smart-contract ownership and deployment. Led a team of engineers to develop a standardized workflow using Safe multisig wallets, Safe Utils, and CREATE3 for deterministic contract deployment across EVM-compatible networks.

- **Established organizational control and long-term ownership of on-chain infrastructure** by requiring smart-contract deployments to originate through multisignature safes with distributed hardware-wallet custody. Designed appropriate signature thresholds and stakeholder distribution to ensure no individual key holder could unilaterally control critical contracts or infrastructure.

- **Created a deterministic, chain-independent deployment framework** using CREATE3, enabling contracts to be deployed to predictable addresses across EVM networks while preserving multisignature ownership and eliminating the need to transfer ownership from individual deployer accounts after deployment.

- **Led the engineering implementation and operational adoption of the deployment architecture**, transitioning the organization toward an exclusive multisignature-based deployment model for production smart contracts and establishing a repeatable foundation for future EVM deployments.

- **Contributed deployment utilities back to the open-source ecosystem**, extending the Safe Utils project with tooling developed through the organization's deployment requirements and ensuring that improvements to the infrastructure could benefit the broader developer community.
