### Chief Technology Officer — Telcoin Autonomous Operations

- **Lead technology strategy and execution** for Telcoin Autonomous Operations (TAO), the operational organization overseen by the nonprofit Telcoin Association, with responsibility for the development, deployment, security, and ongoing operation of the Telcoin technology ecosystem.

- **Oversee the full Telcoin platform technology stack**, spanning the Telcoin Application Network, blockchain infrastructure, on-chain smart contracts, off-chain reward systems, and integrations supporting mobile applications and developers.

- **Lead development of Telcoin Network**, an EVM-compatible Layer 1 blockchain designed to be secured exclusively by GSMA mobile network operators, establishing the foundational settlement and execution infrastructure for the Telcoin ecosystem.

- **Direct blockchain protocol engineering**, including consensus, transaction execution, networking, validator infrastructure, execution infrastructure, data availability, and the interfaces connecting the protocol to the broader Telcoin platform.

- **Oversee TelX**, Telcoin's decentralized finance layer, including liquidity-provider reward distribution, deployment and management of custom liquidity pools, participation monitoring, and eligibility systems supporting decentralized governance.

- **Design and oversee decentralized governance infrastructure**, including NFT-based subscription and eligibility mechanisms used to establish participation rights in protocol governance.

- **Lead and manage the engineering organization**, establishing technical priorities, directing protocol and application development, recruiting engineering talent, and coordinating execution across multiple technical workstreams.

- **Own production-readiness and security strategy**, coordinating third-party security assessments, audit engagements, remediation efforts, and security researchers to validate protocol and application code prior to production deployment.

- **Manage validator and infrastructure-provider strategy**, including identifying, evaluating, interviewing, onboarding, and maintaining relationships with infrastructure and service providers supporting the Telcoin Network validator ecosystem.

- **Lead technical partnership strategy**, overseeing on-chain integrations, infrastructure partnerships, service providers, ecosystem dependencies, and technical requirements necessary to support network adoption and go-to-market initiatives.

- **Drive technical go-to-market strategy** in close coordination with marketing, business development, strategic partners, and ecosystem stakeholders, translating protocol capabilities into deployable products and partnerships.

- **Own technical communications with the Telcoin community**, presenting bi-weekly protocol and network updates during global community meetings and communicating development progress, architectural decisions, milestones, and launch readiness.

- **Provide continuous technical reporting to Telcoin Association council members**, delivering written and verbal updates on protocol development, infrastructure, security, engineering execution, and operational readiness.

- **Oversee technical documentation**, establishing and maintaining documentation covering protocol architecture, infrastructure, developer integrations, operational procedures, and ecosystem participation.

- **Lead network tooling and observability strategy**, including the evaluation and selection of block-explorer infrastructure and other tooling required to make the network accessible, transparent, and operationally maintainable.

- **Oversee application and developer onboarding**, ensuring mobile developers and ecosystem applications can integrate with the Telcoin platform and interact reliably with its underlying blockchain infrastructure.

- **Coordinate cross-functional technical execution across protocol, application, infrastructure, security, partnerships, and operations**, serving as the primary technical owner responsible for moving initiatives from architecture through production deployment.
