### TEL Token Upgrade & Cross-Chain Migration

- **Led the architecture and execution of the TEL token upgrade and migration**, transitioning a non-standard ERC-20 implementation deployed across Ethereum and Polygon into a standardized, future-proof token architecture designed to support TEL as the native asset of the Telcoin Network.

- **Designed and coordinated the migration from a fragmented token architecture**, addressing legacy constraints including non-standard ERC-20 behavior, a two-decimal precision model, separate token addresses across networks, and existing native bridge infrastructure.

- **Owned the end-to-end token migration process**, including architecture, implementation, testing, security assessment, deployment planning, governance coordination, exchange coordination, and decentralized liquidity management across centralized and decentralized markets.

- **Authored and shepherded the governance proposals required to execute the migration**, using the Telcoin Improvement Proposal (TelIP) and Telcoin Governance Improvement Proposal (TGIP) processes to establish decentralized community approval and coordinate implementation across the ecosystem.

- **Designed and implemented a deterministic token contract deployment strategy using CREATE3**, mining the appropriate deployment salt to establish a vanity contract address that could be reproduced consistently across EVM-compatible networks.

- **Ensured the upgraded token contract was deployed exclusively through Safe Utils and multisignature custody**, eliminating single-key ownership and placing contract control behind distributed hardware-wallet security from initial deployment.

- **Designed the upgraded token architecture around a fixed 100 billion TEL supply cap**, establishing mechanisms to maintain, enforce, and independently verify the supply invariant across supported networks.

- **Architected native cross-chain verification and bridging for TEL**, including the native bridge adapter on the Telcoin Network, enabling the Layer 1 to recognize and securely account for representations of TEL originating from external EVM networks.

- **Coordinated centralized exchanges, decentralized exchanges, liquidity providers, and ecosystem participants throughout the migration**, managing the operational sequencing required to maintain market continuity and accurate liquidity during the transition.

- **Established a future-proof token infrastructure for the Telcoin ecosystem**, combining deterministic EVM deployment, distributed hardware-key custody, decentralized governance, cross-chain interoperability, and verifiable supply controls into a unified token architecture.
